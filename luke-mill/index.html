About this collection

Follow the record

These records document actions involving the former Luke Mill property and Port River West. The collection includes agreements, Board of County Commissioners meeting agendas, a closed session notice, property and deed records, and signed documents from county meeting materials.

Presented together, the records allow the public to follow the Commissioners' actions, compare documents from different stages of the process, and review the source material directly.

Questions to keep in mind

What was proposed, when did county officials discuss or approve it, and how do the agreements and public meeting records fit together?
