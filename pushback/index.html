The Pushback Continues

As the commission struggles reluctantly to put open meetings procedures in place, there is some progress. However, they are still pushing back on a full embrace of the public’s role and value to county government. These Public Information Requests qualify as conditional defaults, denying the release of public information. Much of the information would have been in meeting agenda packets, shared at the time that these issues were discussed.
