Maryland Elections

Make a plan to vote!

If you plan to vote for county commissioner, governor, U.S. representative, state senator, state delegate, comptroller, attorney general, sheriff, school board member or other offices on your ballot, make a plan to vote. The information below from the Maryland State Board of Elections can help you be prepared to vote early in person, by mail or at your local polling place on Election Day.

If you're voting by mail, make sure your ballot is postmarked by Nov. 3, 2026. You can also hand-deliver your ballot to an official drop box or your local board of elections by 8 p.m. on Election Day. Find Official Dropboxes ↗

Important Dates and Voter Resources

Tuesday, October 13, 2026

Deadline to Register to Vote Online or by Mail

This is the last day you can register to vote online or by mail. Mail-in registrations must be postmarked on or before October 13, 2026.

Check Your Voter Registration ↗ Register to vote online ↗ Register to vote by mail ↗
Thursday, October 22, 2026

Early Voting Begins

The week ahead of Election Day, you can vote at an early voting center. Polls are open from 7 am to 8 pm. As long as you're in line by 8 pm, you'll be able to vote.

Find early voting location ↗
By Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Deadline To Request a Mail-in Ballot Delivered by Mail or Fax

If you want to receive your mail-in ballot via mail or fax, your request must be received (not just mailed) or submitted online by October 27, 2026.

Request mail-in ballot ↗
Thursday, October 29, 2026

Early Voting Ends

The early voting period in Maryland ends on Oct. 29.

Find early voting location ↗
Friday, October 30, 2026

Deadline To Request a Mail-in Ballot Online

If you want to receive your mail-in ballot via email, submit your request online by Friday, October 30, 2026.

Request a mail-in ballot online ↗
Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Election Day

This is the final day you can vote. Polls are open from 7 am to 8 pm, and in-person registration is available.
